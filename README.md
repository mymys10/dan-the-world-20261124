THE WORLD — Fire Edition: one page summing up Tuesday 24 November
2026: 50 stories across 7 regions. Built by an AI agent for
make.fun's "Sum up 24 November's world news in one image" bounty,
$100 in USDC. The edition word is FIRE: the day's through-line
is fire — the Indonesian candy-factory blaze, tankers burning
in the Strait of Hormuz, and the second Houthi hit on Riyadh's
PIF tower in under a week.

The lead story is the Indonesia factory fire: authorities
confirmed on Tuesday that 26 workers died in Saturday's blaze
at the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia candy plant in Cikande, Serang,
with 8 still missing, and police opened a criminal negligence
probe. GOOD's other three leads are the US Justice Department
seeking forfeiture of over $1.1 billion in crypto stolen by
North Korean hackers from Binance (story 2), the EU presenting
its 22nd sanctions package on Russia (story 18), and Ukraine
claiming its sea drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury off
Libya (story 26).

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the
finished `entry.png` next to it into the output directory
(`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, or `/output`). No network, no
randomness: the same input always writes the same bytes.
The image itself was drawn by a generator script kept outside
this repository, from the 50 verified stories listed below.

## Honesty notes

- Every story below was checked against reporting dated
  24 November 2026. Where an event happened earlier, the
  brief says so: the Merkury attack was Sunday 22/11,
  confirmed Monday 23/11; the EU sanctions package was
  presented Monday 23/11; the DOJ forfeiture was announced
  Monday 23/11; the Megaspeed blacklisting was Monday 23/11;
  the soldier's guilty plea was Monday 23/11; the Villavicencio
  car bomb was Monday night; the preacher's arrest was Sunday
  22/11; the Shenzhou-24 crew was named Tuesday for a Wednesday
  launch; Molly Meldrum died Monday 23/11 and the tributes are
  the Tuesday story.
- The Indonesia toll is 26 dead and 8 still missing, confirmed
  Tuesday 24/11 — not the earlier figure of 24 dead and ~30
  missing that was circulating on Monday.
- The Hormuz tanker story is labelled Iran-reported, not
  independently verified: the IRGC Navy and Iranian Navy say
  four tankers hit mines and caught fire; the UKMTO incident
  on Sunday (three vessels struck by projectiles) is a
  different event.
- The Cartagena hostage story is the Tuesday situation: about
  30 people taken, 5 elderly women freed the same day, 25 still
  held as of 24/11. The Wednesday resolution is not presented
  here as 24/11 news.
- The Shenzhou-24 launch itself happened Wednesday 25/11, so
  only the crew announcement is a 24/11 story.
- No 23/11 story is reused except where a genuine 24/11
  development exists.
